# Privacy Policy

**Land Terrain: Topo & Drainage**

Effective date: 23 November 2026
Developer: Tigran Barseghyan
Contact: **developer.barseghyan@gmail.com**

---

## In short

Land Terrain does not collect any data. There is no account, no analytics, no crash reporting service, no advertising and no tracking. The developer has no server and receives nothing from the app.

Your parcels and everything you save with them stay on your device.

---

## What stays on your device

- the parcels you draw, import or make around where you stand, and their names
- the elevation, stream lines and rainfall figures downloaded for each parcel
- the ponds, profiles, crossings, culverts and watersheds you save
- your settings, such as units and the default grade limit

None of it is sent to the developer or to anyone else. If you use iCloud Backup, your device backup is covered by Apple's terms, not by this policy. Deleting a parcel deletes its data; deleting the app deletes everything it stored.

## Location

Location is used only when you ask for it: to center the map where you are (**Explore here**, drawing your land) and to make a parcel around where you stand. It is processed on the device. The app does not store your location by itself and never sends it to the developer.

If you explore around where you stand, or make a parcel there, that area is downloaded like any other parcel (see the next section), so the request to USGS names an area near you. You can refuse location access and still draw, import and analyse any parcel.

## Network requests

The app's own requests go only to two public U.S. government services, and only to fetch public data:

| Service | What the app sends | Why |
|---|---|---|
| USGS 3D Elevation Program and National Hydrography Dataset (`nationalmap.gov`) | the bounding box of the parcel or area being downloaded | elevation and mapped streams |
| NOAA Hydrometeorological Design Studies Center (`hdsc.nws.noaa.gov`) | one point (latitude and longitude) | rainfall for a creek crossing |

These requests carry no name, no account and no identifier of you or your device beyond what any internet request carries (your IP address, seen by the service). The privacy policies of USGS and NOAA govern those services.

Two parts of iOS also use the network, as in any app that has them:

- **Apple Maps** loads the map and satellite imagery for the area on screen from Apple.
- **The App Store**: buying or restoring Pro, and the App Store pages of the developer's other apps, go through Apple. The developer never receives your payment details or your Apple Account.

## Sharing with the developer's other apps

Land Terrain shares saved parcel boundaries with the developer's other landowner apps (such as Deed to Map) through a file in an App Group container on your device, so a parcel you made in one app can be opened in another. The file never leaves the device.

## Exports and sharing

The PDF report, the map image and the KMZ, KML, GeoJSON, GPX and CSV exports are created on your device. They go only where you send them; the developer receives nothing.

## What the app does not do

No accounts. No analytics or crash reporting services. No advertising. No tracking across apps or websites. No cookies or device fingerprinting. No sale or sharing of data, because none is collected.

The app's App Store privacy label is **Data Not Collected**.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Your rights

Because no personal data is collected, the developer holds nothing about you to access, correct or delete. You control what is on your device: turn location access off at any time in iOS Settings, delete parcels in the app, or delete the app to remove everything it stored.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above changes with it and the updated version appears at this address. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
